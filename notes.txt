olist_orders_dataset
The Parent (1 row per order)
Order ID, Customer ID, order status code, purchase timestamp, approval time, 
and actual/estimated delivery dates.

olist_order_items_dataset
1-to-Many (1+ rows per order)
Individual products bought inside that order, the seller ID, shipping limit date, 
and price breakdown per item.

olist_order_payments_dataset1-to-Many 
(1+ rows per order) 
Payment methods (credit card, voucher, boleto), number of installments, and payment values.

olist_order_reviews_dataset
1-to-Many (1+ rows per order)
Customer satisfaction review scores (1 to 5), comment titles/messages, and submission timestamps.
